What to watch

  • cats and dogs both predict chase and eat. They get the same kind of nudges and end up pointing the same way.
  • mice and balls both predict only chase. fish and meat both predict only eat.
  • Press new random start a few times and jump to 3000. The starting points change every time; the grouping by color comes back every time.
  • Press Step with show the second table on: the green arrow adds a little of the →chase vector to cats, the red arrow subtracts a little of the average guess. Their sum, the black arrow, is the step.
  • With paths on, jump to 100: almost all of the movement happens in the first 20 passes. Cats and dogs end up on the same line from the origin.
  • The model never sees the colors.

Which words are similar?

values:−+ table: one row per word, one column per dimension. Rows in order: animals, chase/eat, things chased, food.

Cosine compares two vectors by their direction only: 1 = same direction, 0 = at a right angle, −1 = opposite. How long the vectors are does not count, so the pictures draw them at the same length.

Words with the same neighbors get the same nudges, so they end up pointing the same way: high cosine. That is how we read similarity off the table.

One-hot vectors never have a 1 in the same place, so any two of them are at a right angle. Cosine 0 for every pair: one-hot vectors cannot say that cats are more like dogs than like fish.
